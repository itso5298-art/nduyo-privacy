This Privacy Policy describes how Nduyo Dzokudira ("the App"), brought to you by Ganyani Khosa, Leornard Mzite, Mallon Itai, and King Godo, handles information when you use our mobile application. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and providing a peaceful, ad-free worship experience for the UCCZ community.

📦 1. Information We Collect

Nduyo Dzokudira is designed to work fully offline and does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information with us, the church, or any third party. Specifically:

  • We do not require account creation or login.
  • We do not collect your name, phone number, email address, or contact details.
  • We do not track your GPS location.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics services.

💾 2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores user data strictly on your device using local storage (AsyncStorage and local device files). This data never leaves your device:

  • Favourite hymns — hymns and choruses you bookmark.
  • Personal notes & service orders — notes and church programs you write.
  • App preferences — font size adjustments and theme state.
  • Practice recordings — audio recordings made during choir or personal hymn practice.

You can reset or clear all locally stored data at any time by clearing the app data or uninstalling the App.

🎙️ 3. Microphone Permission

The App requests access to your device's microphone solely to power the Practice Studio recording feature. Audio recordings are:

  • Saved strictly on your local device storage.
  • Never uploaded to any external server or cloud service.
  • Never shared with third parties.

You may deny microphone permission at any time; all hymn text, liturgical orders, and searching features will continue to work completely normally.

🔗 4. Third-Party Services

The App is built with Expo & React Native. Please review Expo's Privacy Policy for platform details. No advertising networks, social media trackers, or third-party data brokers are included in this application.

👧 5. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages, including children, families, and church youth fellowships. Because the App does not collect any personally identifiable information from any user, there is zero risk of collection from children.

🔄 6. Changes to This Policy

Any updates to this Privacy Policy will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the policy.
