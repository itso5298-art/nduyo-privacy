Nduyo Dzokudira

UCCZ Hymn & Worship App — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 11, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Nduyo Dzokudira ("the App"), developed by Mallonitai's Team, handles information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

📦 1. Information We Collect

Nduyo Dzokudira is designed to work fully offline and does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information with us or any third party. Specifically:

  • We do not require account creation or login.
  • We do not collect your name, email, or contact details.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or analytics services.

💾 2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following data only on your device using AsyncStorage (local storage). This data never leaves your device:

  • Favourite hymns — hymns you mark as favourites.
  • Personal notes — notes you attach to hymns.
  • App preferences — settings and UI state.
  • Audio recordings — practice recordings saved locally.

You can clear all locally stored data at any time by uninstalling the App.

🎙️ 3. Microphone Permission

The App requests access to your device's microphone solely to enable the hymn practice recording feature. Audio recordings are:

  • Saved only on your device.
  • Never uploaded to any server.
  • Never shared with third parties.

You may deny microphone permission; all other features will continue to work normally.

🔗 4. Third-Party Services

The App is built with Expo / React Native. Expo may collect limited, anonymous crash and diagnostic data to improve the platform. Please review Expo's Privacy Policy for details. No other third-party SDKs that collect user data are included.

👧 5. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children or adults. Since no data is collected, there is no risk of inadvertent collection from children under 13.

🔄 6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
